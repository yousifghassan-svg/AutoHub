# AutoHub User Journeys

**Sprint:** Product Strategy 1.0  
**Status:** Draft — awaiting approval  
**Scope:** Product journeys only — not wireframes, not APIs, not React.

These journeys describe the humans AutoHub serves. Every future module should deepen at least one journey without harming the others.

---

## Journey map at a glance

| Actor | Primary job | Success looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Guest Visitor | Explore with low commitment | Remembers AutoHub; returns with intent |
| Buyer | Find and confidently pursue the right vehicle | Contact / visit / decide without regret |
| Seller | List and manage a vehicle with pride | Quality listing live; serious inquiries |
| Dealer | Run inventory and reputation as a business | Leads, presence, repeat buyers |
| Importer | Connect supply to regional demand | Faster placement; clearer pricing signals |
| Marketplace Moderator | Protect trust at scale | Bad supply removed; good supply unharmed |

## 1. Buyer

### Goals

- Find the right vehicle (or plate) quickly — by make, model, city, budget, trust signals.
- Understand condition and price without decoding spam.
- Contact the right seller / dealer with confidence.
- Compare options without drowning.
- Avoid fraud, misrepresentation, and wasted trips.

### Journey stages

1. **Arrive** — From search, share link, brand memory, or dealer profile.  
2. **Orient** — Instant sense that this is a serious automotive place.  
3. **Find** — Search-first; refine when needed.  
4. **Evaluate** — Photography, facts, seller/dealer trust, location, price.  
5. **Act** — Save, message, call, WhatsApp, visit.  
6. **Decide** — Off-platform or future on-platform close; return for next vehicle.

### Pain points

- Noisy listings that look alike and lie differently.  
- Weak photos; buried price; unclear city.  
- Fake urgency and unverified sellers.  
- Filters that feel like homework.  
- Dead ends: sold leftovers, non-responsive sellers.  
- Language / RTL / currency confusion on lesser platforms.

### Opportunities

- Spotlight search + progressive refinement.  
- Premium cards and details that make comparison emotional and clear.  
- Trust marks that mean something (not sticker spam).  
- Saved search and intelligent alerts without nagging.  
- Dealer presence that reduces private-party risk for some buyers.  
- Future: inspection, financing, market price context.

### Moments of delight

- First result feels right within seconds.  
- A listing photo that feels showroom-real.  
- Price and city readable instantly.  
- Messaging that starts in context of the exact car.  
- A dealer profile that feels like a real business.  
- “This app respects me” — calm UI after chaotic classifieds elsewhere.

## 2. Seller (private)

### Goals

- Sell a personal vehicle or plate without becoming a full-time marketer.  
- Create a listing that looks worthy of the car.  
- Understand what “good” looks like before publishing.  
- Manage inquiries and status without chaos.  
- Feel safe and respected.

### Journey stages

1. **Decide to sell** — Motivated by life change, upgrade, or need.  
2. **Start listing** — Enter calmly; draft without fear of losing work.  
3. **Tell the truth** — Specs, price, location, photos.  
4. **Improve quality** — Guidance that helps, not shames.  
5. **Publish / review** — Clear path to live.  
6. **Manage** — Edit, mark sold, respond, pause.  
7. **Complete** — Vehicle gone; leave with dignity.

### Pain points

- Long wizards that feel bureaucratic.  
- Unclear photo standards.  
- Fear of under/over-pricing with no guidance.  
- Spam inquiries and time-wasters.  
- Accidental policy violations.  
- Tools that feel like admin panels, not seller care.

### Opportunities

- Sell wizard as calm final inspection, not checkout panic.  
- Draft engine that never punishes interruption.  
- Quality tips tied to outcomes (better photos → better inquiries).  
- Simple My Listings: status at a glance, one primary action.  
- Future: price intelligence, inspection partners, safe payment rails when trust allows.

### Moments of delight

- “My listing looks premium.”  
- Progress saved when life interrupts.  
- First serious inquiry from a real buyer.  
- One-tap mark sold; inventory stays honest.  
- Guidance that feels like a knowledgeable friend, not a compliance bot.

## 3. Dealer

### Goals

- Publish and refresh inventory efficiently.  
- Present a trustworthy brand presence.  
- Capture and respond to leads quickly.  
- Understand what sells in which city.  
- Scale multi-user operations over time.  
- Be found for the store, not only for single cars.

### Journey stages

1. **Establish presence** — Profile, verification, localization.  
2. **Load inventory** — Bulk or disciplined single-entry quality.  
3. **Merchandize** — Photography standards, pricing, featured placement (ethical).  
4. **Receive demand** — Inquiries across listings.  
5. **Respond & convert** — Speed as reputation.  
6. **Learn** — What gets views, saves, contacts.  
7. **Grow** — Team seats, analytics, partnerships (financing/insurance later).

### Pain points

- Consumer tools that don’t scale to dozens of cars.  
- Inconsistent listing quality across staff.  
- Leads trapped in WhatsApp with no memory.  
- Weak storefront identity on general classifieds.  
- Paying for exposure that trains junk traffic.  
- No regional analytics that match real markets.

### Opportunities

- Dealer platform as a first-class module (not a badge skin).  
- Shared premium card language for inventory.  
- Lead inbox tied to listings.  
- Verification that elevates serious dealers.  
- Analytics: time-to-inquiry, price position, photo quality correlation.  
- Partner rails: financing, inspection, insurance offers that help close.

### Moments of delight

- Storefront that feels like a modern showroom online.  
- Staff publish without breaking brand standards.  
- Buyer arrives already trusting the dealer name.  
- Dashboard that is quiet and useful — Linear, not spreadsheet chaos.  
- Winning a buyer who says “I came because it looked real.”

## 4. Importer

### Goals

- Place imported vehicles into the right regional demand quickly.  
- Understand which specs/markets clear.  
- Coordinate with dealers or sell direct with credibility.  
- Reduce time inventory sits unpriced or unseen.  
- Navigate paperwork/trust realities without platform fiction.

### Journey stages

1. **Source** — Auction, exporter, overseas purchase (often off-platform).  
2. **Land & prepare** — Photos, specs, condition narrative.  
3. **Price** — Dual-currency reality; market comps.  
4. **Distribute** — List on AutoHub; optionally route to dealers.  
5. **Signal** — Read demand; adjust price/placement.  
6. **Clear** — Sell through; repeat with better intelligence.

### Pain points

- Global auction data that doesn’t map to local taste.  
- No clean bridge between import pipeline and local listing quality.  
- Price opacity across cities.  
- Buyers skeptical of import stories without proof.  
- Fragmented tools: Excel + WhatsApp + random classifieds.

### Opportunities

- Vehicle intelligence + market analytics modules.  
- Copart / auction intelligence as decision support — never as spam fodder.  
- Templates for import-ready listings (history, odometer honesty, title state).  
- Dealer wholesale pathways inside the platform later.  
- Trust packaging: inspection, documented provenance.

### Moments of delight

- Knowing what will move before the ship docks.  
- Listing that explains import truth without sounding shady.  
- Faster clear-out in the right city.  
- A repeatable playbook: land → list → lead → close.

## 5. Marketplace Moderator

### Goals

- Protect buyers and honest sellers.  
- Remove fraud, spam, stolen goods signals, and policy abuse.  
- Act fast without harming good supply.  
- Leave a clear audit trail of decisions.  
- Keep policies human-enforceable.

### Journey stages

1. **Detect** — Reports, automated signals, queues.  
2. **Triage** — Severity, evidence, repeat offenders.  
3. **Investigate** — Listing, media, user history, plate/vehicle claims.  
4. **Decide** — Keep, request changes, remove, suspend.  
5. **Communicate** — Clear reasons; appeal path where appropriate.  
6. **Learn** — Patterns feed better prevention (and future AI assist).

### Pain points

- Queues flooded by low-quality reports.  
- Ambiguous policies that create inconsistent judgments.  
- Clever fraud that looks like normal listings.  
- Tools that are slow or hide context.  
- Pressure to “be lenient for growth.”

### Opportunities

- Quality gates upstream so fewer bad listings enter.  
- AI assists that highlight risk — moderator decides.  
- Policy clarity tied to product surfaces (sellers see rules before they fail).  
- Repeat-offender intelligence.  
- Separation of consumer UX calm from moderator power tools.

### Moments of delight

- Catching a scam before many users are hurt.  
- A queue that surfaces true risk first.  
- Policies that are firm and fair.  
- Sellers who improve after clear guidance.  
- Knowing the marketplace feels safer this month than last.

## 6. Guest Visitor

### Goals

- Understand what AutoHub is in seconds.  
- Browse without creating an account wall.  
- Feel the premium difference vs general classifieds.  
- Decide whether to trust enough to return or register.  
- Share a listing with someone else.

### Journey stages

1. **Land** — Homepage, deep link, shared listing, SEO.  
2. **Feel** — Brand + photography + clarity (memory test).  
3. **Browse** — Search and scroll without friction.  
4. **Hit a gate** — Soft: save/contact may invite auth; never ambush.  
5. **Return or convert** — Come back with intent; or become buyer/seller.

### Pain points

- Aggressive login walls.  
- Generic first impression (“another classifieds site”).  
- Clutter that signals low trust.  
- Broken mobile experience.  
- Unclear whether AutoHub is Iraq-only, regional, or noisy global.

### Opportunities

- Homepage as showroom door (brand-first, search-first).  
- Guest-complete browse of public supply.  
- Elegant auth moment only when value requires identity.  
- Localization that feels native on first visit.  
- Content that teaches trust without a lecture.

### Moments of delight

- “This doesn’t look like OpenSooq.”  
- Finding a beautiful listing as a guest.  
- Sharing a clean vehicle link.  
- Theme and language that feel considered.  
- Wanting an account because the product earned it — not because it trapped them.

## Success across journeys

We are winning when:

- Guests remember AutoHub tomorrow.  
- Buyers feel informed, not hustled.  
- Sellers feel proud of their listing.  
- Dealers run a real book of business.  
- Importers waste less time on wrong markets.  
- Moderators protect trust faster than abuse invents new shapes.

**If any journey is improved by harming trust, the improvement is invalid.**
